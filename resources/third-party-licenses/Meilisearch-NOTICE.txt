The binary is a statically linked Rust executable and embeds a large number of
transitive crates under permissive licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD). They are not
enumerated here: the complete dependency set with exact versions is fixed by the
Cargo.lock of the pinned upstream tag above, from which the corresponding source
of every crate is reachable.

No source modifications are applied by this product: the binary is downloaded
from the upstream URL recorded in scripts/versions.json and verified against the
SHA-256 pinned in that same file.

Note on licensing scope: this product ships Meilisearch under the MIT license as
published for v1.13.0. It does not use, enable or ship any Meilisearch
Enterprise Edition component.
